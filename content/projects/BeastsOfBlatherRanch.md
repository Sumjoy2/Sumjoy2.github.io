### Long

> [!IMPORTANT] Name Discrepancy
> From now on I will be refering to *Beasts of Blather Ranch* simply by *Blather Ranch*

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; The capstone project that I was a part of at **The Academy of Interactive Entertainment** is Blather Ranch. This was a collaborative effort between the other disciplines of Art, Game Design, and Programming (that's me!). I was able to get some remote experience on this project because the Seattle campus was working with the Lafayette campus. With a total of about 16 people working on it.

---

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Most of what I outlined above is work on the 3rd and closing minigame centered around a crystal bat. The bat minigame is a bug eating, obstacle avoiding, spline following minigame. You control the bat eating "Good Bugs" and avoiding both Spiders & environment that will harm the bat. Flying to the end of this minigame brings you to the end of the game.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Since this is a very spline heavy minigame I created a couple spline tools. Some of the tools include a component to follow a specific spline both for internal & external splines, the ability to spawn *Actors* between set percents of the spline, and spawning *Static Meshes* along the spline. A lot of this was needed for the minigame to work. A bonus to having made all these spline tools is it was able to be used with the Dog's flight path.
